From reinforcement learning to preference optimization

12 practical workshops · 90 minutes each · one focal algorithm per meeting · 23 teaching weeks plus onboarding and capstone review = 5–6 months.

Biweekly meetings leave 2–4 hours per week for reading, implementation and longer experiments. No GPU, paid API or model downloads are required for the core pack.

Schedule

Teaching progression: first use your working CartPole loop, then Blackjack and Taxi lookup tables. No neural networks in sessions 1–4. Session 5 explains why continuous observations motivate a linear approximator; session 6 introduces DQN’s neural value model.

Optional extensions

4A: n-step SARSA bridge. 4B: value iteration on FrozenLake. 5A: tile coding. 6A: Double DQN and replay ablations. 8A: dedicated actor-critic workshop before PPO. 8B: GAE and vectorized PPO. 9A: sequence models, pretraining and response masks. 11A: verifier attacks and group-size ablation. Add 2–4 meetings for learners new to neural networks; this extends the calendar beyond six months unless meetings become weekly.

MuJoCo continuous control follows PPO; Box2D follows reliable CartPole training; ALE/Atari follows DQN with convolution, preprocessing and larger replay. These are optional transfers, not 90-minute full-training promises.
